💡 **What:** Replaced `.into_iter().flatten()` and `.into_iter().flatten().cloned()` on `Option` types with explicit `if let Some` blocks. 🎯 **Why:** Iterating over an `Option` to flatten it is inefficient compared to directly unboxing it and iterating the inner collection. 📊 **Measured Improvement:** The `flatten()` vs `if let Some()` pattern overhead reduction was measured locally via a microbenchmark but the primary effect is on reducing unnecessary iterator allocations, ensuring cleaner unboxed iterators for queue resolution.
